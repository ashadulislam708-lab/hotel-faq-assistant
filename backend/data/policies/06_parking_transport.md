# Parking & Transport Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## Is parking available on-site?

Yes, the hotel has a self-park garage attached to the main building with 120 spaces reserved for hotel guests.

## What is the cost of parking?

Self-parking is $18 per night. Valet parking is available for $28 per night, including unlimited in-and-out access. Day-visitor parking (non-overnight) is $10 for up to 4 hours and $15 for a full day.

## Is oversized vehicle parking available?

Vehicles over 7 feet in height (SUVs, vans, trucks) can be accommodated in the garage's designated tall-vehicle spaces on the ground level. Guests with trailers or oversized vehicles should call ahead to confirm availability, as space is limited.

## Do you offer electric vehicle charging?

Yes, 6 EV charging stations are available in the garage on a first-come, first-served basis at no additional cost beyond the standard parking fee.

## Is there an airport shuttle?

A complimentary shuttle runs between the hotel and Harborview Regional Airport (15 minutes away) every 30 minutes from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Reservations for the shuttle can be made at the front desk or by calling the hotel at least 2 hours in advance.

## Is transportation available outside shuttle hours?

Guests arriving or departing outside shuttle hours can arrange a taxi or rideshare pickup through the front desk, or use the designated rideshare pickup/drop-off zone at the main entrance.

## Is there public transit nearby?

The Harborview Transit Center, with bus and light rail connections, is a 5-minute walk from the hotel. Front desk staff can provide current route maps and schedules.

## Can I request a rental car be delivered to the hotel?

Yes, the hotel partners with two local rental agencies that offer delivery and pickup directly at the main entrance. Ask the concierge desk to arrange a booking.

## Is overnight parking guaranteed if I arrive late?

Self-park garage spaces are held for all registered overnight guests regardless of arrival time; valet parking is subject to valet staffing hours (6:00 AM–1:00 AM). Guests arriving after 1:00 AM should use self-parking or contact the front desk for assistance.
