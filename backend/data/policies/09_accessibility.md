# Accessibility Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## Are accessible rooms available?

Yes, the hotel offers ADA-compliant accessible rooms featuring roll-in showers or accessible tubs with grab bars, lowered fixtures and counters, and wider doorways. Accessible rooms can be requested at booking or by contacting reservations directly.

## How do I reserve an accessible room?

Select "Accessible Room" as the room type during online booking, or request one by phone or email. Because the number of accessible rooms is limited, early booking is recommended, especially during peak season.

## What mobility equipment is available on request?

The hotel can provide, subject to availability: shower chairs, bed rails, raised toilet seats, and manual wheelchairs for on-property use. Requests should be made at least 48 hours before arrival to guarantee availability.

## Is the hotel wheelchair accessible throughout?

Yes, the main entrance, lobby, elevators, guest floors, dining venues, pool, fitness center, and business center are all wheelchair accessible via ramps and accessible-width doorways. Accessible parking spaces are available near the main entrance at no additional charge beyond standard parking fees.

## Are service animals permitted for guests with disabilities?

Yes, trained service animals are welcome throughout the hotel, including dining venues, at no charge and without the pet policy's fees or restrictions. See the Pet Policy for full details.

## Do you provide accommodations for guests who are deaf or hard of hearing?

Rooms equipped with visual alert kits (door knock alert, phone alert, and fire alarm strobe light) are available on request. TTY-compatible phone service can also be arranged through the front desk.

## Are accessible features available in meeting and event spaces?

Yes, all meeting rooms and the main event space are wheelchair accessible, with accessible restrooms nearby and assistive listening devices available on request for hosted events.

## Who can I contact for accessibility questions before booking?

Guests with specific accessibility needs are encouraged to email access@harborviewinn.example.com or call the front desk directly before booking to confirm a room or arrangement meets their requirements.
