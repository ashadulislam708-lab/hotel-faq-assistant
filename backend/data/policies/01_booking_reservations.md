# Booking & Reservations Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## How can I make a reservation?

Reservations can be made through the following channels:

- The hotel website booking engine at harborviewinn.example.com
- By phone at (555) 240-1188, daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM local time
- By email at reservations@harborviewinn.example.com
- Through approved third-party travel and booking platforms

## What information is required to book a room?

A valid credit or debit card, the guest's full name, expected arrival and departure dates, and the number of guests per room are required to complete a booking. A government-issued photo ID matching the reservation name is required at check-in.

## Is a deposit required?

A one-night room deposit is charged to the card on file at the time of booking to guarantee the reservation. The deposit is applied toward the final bill. For stays of 5 nights or longer, a deposit equal to 50% of the total estimated stay is required.

## What room types are available?

- Standard Queen Room
- Standard Double Queen Room
- Deluxe King Room
- Business King Suite (includes work desk and lounge access)
- Accessible Rooms (see Accessibility Policy for details)

## Is there a minimum age to book or check in?

The primary guest on any reservation must be at least 18 years of age and present at check-in.

## Can I modify my reservation after booking?

Reservations can be modified online, by phone, or by email up until 48 hours before the scheduled arrival date at no charge, subject to room availability at the new dates. Modifications within 48 hours of arrival are treated as a new booking and are subject to the standard Cancellation & Refund Policy.

## Do you offer group or corporate rates?

Group bookings of 10 rooms or more, and corporate accounts with a signed rate agreement, receive discounted nightly rates. Contact groups@harborviewinn.example.com at least 14 days before the event or stay to arrange group billing and a group booking contract.

## Is my rate guaranteed once booked?

Yes. The nightly rate confirmed at the time of booking is guaranteed and will not change due to later rate fluctuations, except in cases of reservation modification, promotional rate expiration, or applicable tax/fee changes mandated by local authorities.

## Do you require full prepayment?

Standard reservations are not prepaid in full; the deposit is charged at booking and the balance is charged at check-out. Non-refundable "Advance Purchase" rate bookings are charged in full at the time of booking and are not eligible for refund or date changes.
