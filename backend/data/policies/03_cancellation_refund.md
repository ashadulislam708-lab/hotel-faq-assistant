# Cancellation & Refund Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## What is the standard cancellation window?

Standard rate reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in date (before 3:00 PM local time, two days prior to arrival).

## What happens if I cancel within 48 hours of arrival?

Cancellations made within 48 hours of the check-in date are charged a fee equal to one night's room rate plus applicable taxes. The remaining deposit, if any, is refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## What is the no-show policy?

Guests who do not arrive and do not cancel by the check-in date are considered a "no-show." The full first night's rate plus applicable taxes is charged, and the remainder of the reservation is released and cancelled without further penalty.

## Are "Advance Purchase" or non-refundable rates cancellable?

No. Non-refundable "Advance Purchase" rate bookings are paid in full at booking and are not eligible for cancellation, refund, or date changes for any reason, including early departure.

## Can I get a refund if I leave early?

Early departure after check-in is treated as a same-stay change. Guests on standard rates are refunded for unused nights only if they notify the front desk before 11:00 AM on the day of the intended early departure. Non-refundable rate bookings are not eligible for early-departure refunds.

## How are refunds issued?

Refunds are issued to the original form of payment used for booking. Credit card refunds typically post within 5–7 business days; refunds to third-party booking platforms follow that platform's own refund timeline, which may take up to 14 business days.

## Can group or event bookings be cancelled under the standard policy?

Group bookings (10+ rooms) and event bookings follow the cancellation terms specified in the signed group contract, which may include a longer notice window (typically 14–30 days) and a tiered cancellation fee schedule.

## What if I need to cancel due to an emergency?

The hotel reviews documented emergencies (e.g., medical emergency, bereavement, government travel restriction) on a case-by-case basis. Guests should contact reservations@harborviewinn.example.com with supporting documentation to request a policy exception; approval is at the hotel's discretion and is not guaranteed.

## Does travel insurance affect this policy?

The hotel's cancellation policy applies regardless of any third-party travel insurance the guest may hold. Guests seeking reimbursement for cancellation fees through their insurance provider should retain the hotel's cancellation confirmation and fee receipt for their claim.
