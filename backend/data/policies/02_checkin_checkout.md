# Check-In / Check-Out Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## What time is check-in?

Standard check-in time is 3:00 PM local time. Rooms cannot be guaranteed before this time, though early arrivals are welcome to store luggage at the front desk.

## What time is check-out?

Standard check-out time is 11:00 AM local time.

## Can I request an early check-in?

Early check-in is offered on a subject-to-availability basis. Guests may request early check-in at booking or by contacting the front desk. Confirmed early check-in (before 12:00 PM) incurs a fee of $25; check-in between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM is complimentary when a room is ready.

## Can I request a late check-out?

Late check-out until 1:00 PM is available free of charge, subject to availability, and can be requested at the front desk on the morning of departure. Late check-out between 1:00 PM and 6:00 PM incurs a fee equal to 50% of the nightly rate. Departure after 6:00 PM is billed as an additional full night.

## What do I need to present at check-in?

A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID card) matching the name on the reservation, and the credit or debit card used to guarantee the booking. A second form of ID may be requested if the presented card is not in the guest's name.

## Is a security deposit required at check-in?

An incidental hold of $50 per night is authorized on the guest's card at check-in to cover incidentals such as room service, minibar use, or damages. This hold is released within 3–7 business days after check-out, depending on the card issuer.

## How many keys/key cards do I receive?

Each reservation includes two key cards per room at no charge. Additional or replacement key cards are $5 each.

## What happens if I arrive after the front desk closes?

The front desk operates 24 hours a day, so there is no cutoff for arrival. Guests arriving after 11:00 PM are asked to call the hotel directly upon arrival if the main entrance is locked, so staff can grant access.

## What if I need to check out but the front desk is busy?

Express check-out is available via the in-room folio slip, the guest email on file, or the hotel mobile app. The final bill is emailed automatically by 12:00 PM on the day of departure.
