# Pet Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, Harborview Inn & Suites is pet-friendly. Dogs and cats are welcome in designated pet-friendly rooms, subject to availability and advance notice at the time of booking.

## Is there a pet fee?

A non-refundable pet fee of $50 per stay (up to 2 pets) applies. Stays longer than 7 nights are charged an additional $10 per night for pets from night 8 onward.

## Are there restrictions on pet size or breed?

A maximum of 2 pets per room is allowed, with a combined weight limit of 75 lbs. The hotel does not have breed restrictions but reserves the right to refuse accommodation for any animal displaying aggressive behavior toward staff or other guests.

## Do service animals require the pet fee?

No. Trained service animals, as defined under applicable disability access laws, are always welcome at no charge and are not subject to the pet fee, size limit, or room restrictions. Emotional support animals that are not trained service animals are subject to the standard pet policy and fee.

## Can I leave my pet unattended in the room?

Pets may be left unattended in the room for short periods (e.g., a meal at the restaurant) if crated or otherwise contained, and if housekeeping is notified via the door hanger provided. Pets may not be left unattended for extended periods (e.g., a full day) or in a manner that causes noise disturbance to other guests.

## Are pets allowed in dining and common areas?

Pets are not permitted in indoor dining venues, the pool area, or the fitness center, in accordance with health regulations. Pets are welcome in the lobby, on outdoor patios, and in guest rooms. A designated pet relief area is located at the north side of the parking garage.

## What happens if pet-related damage occurs?

Any damage caused by a pet (chewing, stains, odor requiring deep cleaning) will be charged to the guest's card on file, up to a maximum of $300 per incident, in addition to the standard pet fee.

## Do you provide pet amenities?

Yes, on request the front desk provides a pet bed, food and water bowls, and waste bags at no additional charge, subject to availability.
