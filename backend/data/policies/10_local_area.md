# Local Area Information — Harborview Inn & Suites

## How far is the hotel from the airport?

Harborview Regional Airport (HVR) is approximately 8 miles / 15 minutes from the hotel by car or the complimentary hotel shuttle (see Parking & Transport Policy).

## What is within walking distance of the hotel?

- Harborview Waterfront Promenade — 5-minute walk
- Harborview Transit Center (bus/light rail) — 5-minute walk
- Downtown Shopping District — 10-minute walk
- City Convention Center — 12-minute walk

## What are some notable local attractions?

- **Harborview Maritime Museum** — 10 minutes by car, exhibits on the region's shipping history
- **Cascade Park** — 15-minute walk, walking trails and a public garden
- **Harborview Farmers Market** — Saturdays, 8:00 AM–1:00 PM, at the Waterfront Promenade
- **The Old Lighthouse District** — historic shops and cafés, 10 minutes by car

## Does the hotel offer tours or activity bookings?

The concierge desk can arrange tickets and reservations for local tours, the maritime museum, harbor cruises, and area restaurants. Requests are best made at least 24 hours in advance for popular activities.

## What is the best way to get around the local area?

The Harborview Transit Center offers bus and light rail service across the city. Rideshare and taxi pickup is available at the main entrance. Bicycle rentals are available at the Waterfront Promenade kiosk, a 5-minute walk from the hotel.

## Are there hospitals or urgent care nearby?

Harborview General Hospital is located 1.5 miles from the hotel (approximately 6 minutes by car). An urgent care clinic is located within Cascade Park Plaza, a 5-minute drive away. Front desk staff can call for emergency transport if needed.

## What is the local weather like / what should I pack?

Harborview has a temperate coastal climate: mild, wet winters (average 45–55°F / 7–13°C) and warm, dry summers (average 70–82°F / 21–28°C). Guests visiting between November and March are advised to pack a waterproof jacket, as rain is common.

## Is the hotel near any major event venues?

Yes, the City Convention Center is a 12-minute walk, and the Harborview Arena (concerts and sporting events) is 10 minutes by car or a short rideshare trip. Guests attending events at either venue are encouraged to book early during peak event dates.
