# Wifi & Tech Support Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## Is wifi available at the hotel?

Yes, complimentary high-speed wifi is available throughout guest rooms, meeting spaces, and all public areas, including the lobby, café, and pool deck.

## How do I connect to the wifi?

Select the network "Harborview-Guest" and enter the access code printed on the guest room key card sleeve, or provided at check-in. The connection covers up to 4 devices per room at no charge.

## Is there a faster or premium wifi tier?

Standard complimentary wifi supports browsing, email, and video calls. A premium high-bandwidth tier ($9.95/day) is available for guests needing faster speeds for large file transfers or 4K streaming, purchasable through the in-room portal.

## What if I have trouble connecting to wifi?

Guests can call the front desk (dial 0) or IT support (dial 55) from any in-room phone for troubleshooting, available 24 hours a day. Common fixes include reconnecting to the network and re-entering the access code, which resets each day at noon for security.

## Can I print or access a computer without a laptop?

Yes, the business center on the ground floor offers two guest workstations with internet access, Microsoft Office, and printing, available 24 hours daily using a room key card.

## Are charging cables or adapters available if I forget mine?

The front desk keeps a small stock of complimentary loaner charging cables (USB-C, Lightning, Micro-USB) and international plug adapters, available on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Is wifi available on the airport shuttle?

Yes, the airport shuttle is equipped with complimentary wifi for guest use during transit.

## Does the hotel offer smart TV or streaming service access in rooms?

All rooms include a smart TV with access to major streaming apps. Guests can log into their personal streaming accounts during their stay; the hotel automatically signs guests out of all accounts at check-out for privacy and security.
