# Dining Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## Is breakfast included in my room rate?

Complimentary continental breakfast is included with all Standard and Deluxe room bookings, served in the Harborview Café from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM daily. Business King Suite guests also receive access to the express hot breakfast buffet. Guests on discounted or promotional rates should check their booking confirmation, as breakfast inclusion may vary by rate plan.

## What on-site dining options are available?

- **Harborview Café** — breakfast and casual all-day dining, open 6:30 AM–10:00 PM
- **The Pier Grill** — full-service restaurant for lunch and dinner, open 11:30 AM–10:00 PM, reservations recommended for parties of 6 or more
- **Lobby Bar** — drinks and light bar snacks, open 4:00 PM–midnight

## Is room service available?

Room service is available daily from 6:30 AM to 11:00 PM. Orders can be placed by dialing extension 0 from the in-room phone or via the hotel mobile app. A $5 delivery fee and 18% service charge apply to all room service orders.

## Can dietary restrictions and allergies be accommodated?

Yes. All on-site dining venues offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free menu options. Guests with food allergies should inform their server or note allergies when booking, and the culinary team can prepare accommodations with advance notice of at least 2 hours for special requests.

## Is there a dress code for the restaurant?

The Harborview Café and Lobby Bar are casual, no dress code. The Pier Grill requests smart-casual attire for dinner service (collared shirts, no swimwear or beach attire).

## Can I bring outside food or drinks into the hotel restaurants?

Outside food and beverages are not permitted in on-site dining venues, with the exception of store-bought baby food and medically necessary dietary items.

## Do you offer catering for private events?

Yes, the hotel offers in-house catering for meetings, celebrations, and private events held in our function rooms. Contact events@harborviewinn.example.com at least 7 days in advance to arrange a menu and quote.

## Is there a minibar in the room?

Deluxe King Rooms and Business King Suites include an in-room minibar stocked with snacks and beverages, billed automatically to the room upon use. Minibar items can be checked at check-out to confirm charges before departure.

## What are the charges for children's dining?

Children aged 5 and under eat free from the children's menu when accompanied by a paying adult at any on-site restaurant. Children aged 6–12 receive a 50% discount on children's menu items.
