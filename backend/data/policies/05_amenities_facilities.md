# Amenities & Facilities Policy — Harborview Inn & Suites

## What amenities are available on-site?

- 24-hour fitness center
- Indoor heated swimming pool (open 6:00 AM–10:00 PM)
- Business center with printing, scanning, and 2 workstations
- Guest laundry room (coin and app-operated) and same-day laundry/dry cleaning service
- Meeting rooms and event space (capacity up to 80 guests)
- Vending and ice machines on every floor

## Is the fitness center accessible 24 hours?

Yes, the fitness center is accessible 24/7 using a guest's room key card. Guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Is there a fee to use the pool or fitness center?

No, pool and fitness center access are complimentary for all registered hotel guests. Day passes for non-guests are not offered.

## Is laundry service available?

Same-day laundry and dry-cleaning service is available; bags and order forms are provided in each room. Orders placed before 9:00 AM are returned by 6:00 PM the same day. The self-service guest laundry room is available 24 hours and accepts both coins and the hotel mobile app for payment.

## Can I use the business center to print documents?

Yes, the business center offers printing (black & white and color), scanning, and faxing. The first 10 black & white pages per stay are complimentary; additional pages and all color printing are billed per page at checkout.

## Do you offer luggage storage?

Complimentary luggage storage is available at the front desk before check-in and after check-out, for same-day use.

## Is there an elevator / lift?

Yes, all guest floors are served by two elevators. In the rare event of elevator maintenance, staff will assist guests with accessibility needs via the service elevator.

## Do you offer a shoe-shine, ironing board, or iron in rooms?

An iron and ironing board are provided in every room. Guests may also request a complimentary pressing service for up to 2 garments through housekeeping.

## Is smoking allowed anywhere on the property?

Harborview Inn & Suites is a smoke-free property, including all guest rooms, indoor common areas, and the pool area. Designated outdoor smoking areas are located at the rear parking entrance. A cleaning fee of $250 applies for smoking in a non-designated area.
